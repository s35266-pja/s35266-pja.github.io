Tarot cards

Tarot Cards provide buffs and boons to The Lamb. Each Tarot Card's effect stacks with one another, however, you cannot have two of the same card at once. Cards are found in chests, can be received from Clauneck, and from certain fleeces. Tarot card can have a + or ++ modifier which greatly increases their effects.

The Hearts I

A minor remedy

Gain an extra Half red heart half red heart (no + or ++)

Soul Snatcher

One's loss is the gain of another.

When killing an enemy 10% (20%) chance of healing Half red heart half red heart

Neptune's Curse

Send 'em to sleep with the fishes.

Enemies have a chance to drop a fish. (no + or ++)

Rabbit's Foot

Feels lucky ...and fluffy.

Increases the chance to spawn better chests. (no + or ++)

Usurper Blessed

Hark! Ender of eras, child of fortune!

Heal fully when entering a boss room.

The Hoarder

Hold on, hold on, hold on.

Heal when a Relic is equipped, take damage when a Relic is used. You lose a Half red heart half red heart

The Bellwether

The bell calls the flock together.

Summon a ghost that distracts enemies.

The Vanquisher

A siege unrelenting.

When an enemy is killed, 10% chance of striking a random enemy with lightning.
