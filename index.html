About

Cult of the Lamb casts players as a possessed lamb, saved from untimely execution by a mysterious and ominous deity known as The One Who Waits. To repay this immense cosmic debt, you must build a loyal community of woodland worshippers in a sprawling, dangerous land ruled by powerful false prophets. The game brilliantly juxtaposes a cute, colorful cartoon art style with dark, occult themes, creating a unique and captivating atmosphere where adorable animals engage in grim, dark rituals. By venturing into randomly generated biomes, you will engage in fast-paced, hack-and-slash combat, fighting off hordes of relentless enemies and defeating the four rival cult leaders to expand your supreme influence. Between these dangerous crusades, the gameplay shifts into a deep colony simulator. You return to your village to manage your growing community, collect valuable resources, construct new buildings, and perform dark rituals. Delivering daily sermons is essential to reinforce your flock's faith, change their doctrines, and ensure they remain completely loyal. Your ultimate journey is one of survival, strategy, and power as you rise from a sacrificial lamb to become the ultimate and true lamb god.

Developer

Massive Monster is an independent game development studio based across Australia, the UK, and Singapore. Cult of the Lamb came from a desire to blend the fast-paced, just-one-more-go gameplay of the roguelike genre with the longer-term, meditative gameplay found in base building and colony simulators. As huge fans of both genres, Massive Monster wanted to find something new and unique by combining them, highlighting the differences in pace and gameplay by juxtaposing their bright and colorful cartoon artwork with darker horror elements. The team focused heavily on creating a seamless transition between the intense dungeon crawling and the relaxing camp management, ensuring that every action in one half directly benefits your progress in the other. Nearly four years of passionate development later, Cult of the Lamb is the spectacular result of their hard work! Massive Monster hopes you enjoy exploring this cute-but-creepy world, and they continue to support the game with major free updates, expanding the lore, adding new features, and keeping the community engaged.

Gameplay

Cult of the Lamb features an addictive hybrid gameplay loop that perfectly balances action-packed exploration with strategic base management. Players venture into randomly generated dungeons across four distinct regions, armed with mystical weapons and powerful tarot card buffs to fight through hordes of heretics and monstrous bosses. Back at the commune, the game shifts into a deep colony simulator where you must build shelters, manage resources, cook meals, and perform dark rituals to keep your followers happy. Neglecting your cult can lead to dissent, sickness, or outright rebellion, forcing you to use strict discipline or kind rewards to maintain order. When you need a well-deserved break from the frantic combat and management, you can visit the Lonely Shack to play Knucklebones, a tactical dice mini-game. This high-stakes activity allows you to challenge unique NPCs, test your strategy, and win extra gold to fund your cult's expansion. Balancing these two contrasting halves is the key to successfully growing your flock and conquering the old faith.

Story

Cult of the Lamb delivers a dark yet captivating narrative centered around betrayal, destiny, and cosmic vengeance. The story begins with the tragic execution of the last remaining lamb in the Land of the Old Faith, sacrificed by four tyrannical bishop deities to prevent a powerful prophecy. However, instead of passing into oblivion, the dead lamb is pulled into a purgatorial realm where it meets The One Who Waits—a mysterious, chained god who was betrayed and imprisoned by those very same bishops. The ancient entity offers the lamb a sinister bargain: return to life with godlike powers in exchange for starting a loyal cult in his name and freeing him from his eternal bonds. Accepted into service, the resurrected lamb is granted the Red Crown and sent back to the mortal world. Your holy crusade is to hunt down the four false prophets, dismantle their ancient heretical factions, and ultimately decide whether to remain a faithful servant or claim the ultimate cosmic throne for yourself.
